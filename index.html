Call-to-Site-Visit Lead Card

A small prototype I tried for a real-estate AI voice agent: when a call ends, turn the transcript into a structured lead card a sales manager can act on in 10 seconds (budget, configuration, locality, site-visit slot), score it, and draft the WhatsApp follow-up. Call-ended webhooks are processed idempotently, so a redelivered event never creates a duplicate lead or a second WhatsApp message. All data here is mock data.
